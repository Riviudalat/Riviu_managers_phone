validation, decoded and authenticated MJPEG, clipboard byte equality, Unicode

candidate must be activated in place, retain the same PID, and return that same
bundle/PID from `/wda/activeAppInfo` before set/get can count as byte-exact evidence.
Pending device evidence is 50 causal taps and 20 causal swipes against defined
Settings regions with no-action controls, plus five minutes of MJPEG at at least
one frame/second, no gap above two seconds, at most one reconnect, and protected
health/session checks every five seconds. A check cycle above five seconds,
completion gap above 5.5 seconds, or schedule lateness above 0.5 seconds fails
instead of being recovered by clustered catch-up checks.
